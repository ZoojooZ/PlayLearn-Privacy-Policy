PlayLearn Privacy Policy

This policy covers every edition of PlayLearn: all languages, on Android and on iPhone and iPad.

Effective 26 October 2026

In short

  • PlayLearn has no ads, no analytics and no trackers.
  • We do not run any servers and we do not collect your personal data.
  • Your progress and scores stay on your device.
  • Android editions with a worldwide leaderboard offer an optional Google Play Games sign-in. Google, not us, handles that data.

Who we are

PlayLearn is made by Zoojooz (“we”, “us”), an independent developer. You can reach us at zoojjjooz@gmail.com.

What this policy covers

PlayLearn is published in several languages (for example PlayLearn English), on Google Play and the Apple App Store. All editions are covered by this one policy. Editions differ in language, and in whether they include the optional Google Play Games features described below, which exist on Android only.

Data stored on your device

PlayLearn saves your game progress, high scores and settings (such as language, sound and music) on your device. In the iPhone and iPad edition this also includes any player names and avatars you create. This data is kept in the app’s own local storage. It is never sent to us. It is removed when you uninstall the app, or when you clear the app’s data on Android.

Google Play Games (Android, optional)

Android editions that include leaderboards or achievements let you sign in with Google Play Games. Signing in is optional. If you decline, or you are offline, the game works normally and your progress stays on your device.

If you do sign in, Google’s Play Games Services SDK is used to:

  • identify your Play Games profile (gamer name and avatar) so scores can be shown under it;
  • submit your best score to the worldwide leaderboard; and
  • unlock and show achievements.

This data is collected and processed by Google under Google’s own terms and privacy policy. Data is encrypted in transit. You control who can see your Play Games profile, and you can delete this data, from your Play Games profile or your Google Account. We do not receive or store it.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, device identifiers or advertising identifiers. We do not ask you to create an account with us. There are no in-app purchases.

Ads, analytics and tracking

PlayLearn shows no advertisements and contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting libraries of our own. We do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not sell or share personal data.

Stores and other third parties

Google Play and the Apple App Store process your download and may collect information under their own policies. We do not receive personal data from them. Apart from Google Play Games (above), PlayLearn does not send data to any third party.

Children

PlayLearn is for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has given personal information through the optional Google sign-in, please contact us, and see Google’s guidance on managing a child’s Google Account.

Keeping and deleting data

We hold no personal data, so there is nothing for us to keep or delete. To remove data on your device, uninstall the app. To remove Play Games data, use your Play Games profile or Google Account.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK or California), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. We hold none, so there is nothing for us to provide, but you are welcome to contact us with any request or question. For Play Games data, Google is responsible and handles requests through your Google Account.

Languages

This policy is published in English. Any translation is offered for convenience, and the English version applies if the two differ.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, for example because a new feature involves data, we will post the new version on this page with a new effective date.
